About me

My route into technology started in finance. At Infomate and John Keells IT, part of John Keells Holdings, I worked in SAP FICO, handling invoicing, reconciliations and financial reporting. I started automating repetitive work with Microsoft Power Automate, and the hours it saved showed me how much value good software creates.

That led me to study software engineering and then data science. Today I build full-stack web applications at CoreNova Tech and part-time at Noodlecode. I also work part-time as a Home Network Consultant for TP-Link network products and install smart home systems, from smart switches to smart accessories. My final-year project, RecruitIQ, uses natural language processing to screen resumes and match candidates to jobs.

I am most interested in machine learning, NLP, computer vision, robotics and IoT. My next step is an internship in machine learning, AI or data science, followed by a Master's in AI/ML Engineering in Germany.
